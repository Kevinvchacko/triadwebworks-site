Questions people ask

Something missing? Ask us on the free call.

Which payment gateways can you set up?

We integrate the gateway that fits your business, such as Razorpay or Stripe, including UPI and cards where the gateway supports them. If you prefer, orders can also go straight to WhatsApp for you to confirm and collect payment.

Should I use Shopify or a custom store?

It depends on your products, budget and how much control you want. We'll explain the trade-offs on the free call and recommend the option that fits, rather than the one that's easiest for us.

Can I add and edit products myself?

Yes. You'll be able to add products, change prices and update stock yourself. We'll walk you through it at launch.

How long does an e-commerce website take?

Stores take longer than a simple business site because of payments, products and testing. We give you a timeline after the free call, once we know how many products and features you need.

Do you help after the store goes live?

Yes. Our Managed Care plan covers updates, fixes, and speed and SEO check-ups, so the store keeps working while you focus on orders.
