Questions people ask

Something missing? Ask us on the free call.

How much does a website cost in Chennai?

It depends on how many pages you need and what the site has to do, such as bookings, payments or a menu. We quote every project after a free call, and the written quote lists exactly what is included, so you only pay for what you need.

How long does it take to build a business website?

A focused business website usually takes a few weeks from the first call to launch. Sites with online stores, bookings or custom features take longer. You get a timeline on the free call.

Will my website show up on Google?

Every site we build includes SEO basics: keyword-focused titles and descriptions, structured data, a sitemap and fast loading. We also help you set up Google Search Console and a Google Business Profile, which matter most for local searches in Chennai.

Can I update the website myself?

Yes. We set it up so you can change text, photos, prices and hours yourself. If you'd rather not, our Managed Care plan covers small updates every month.

Can you redesign the website we already have?

Yes. We can redesign an existing site, move your content across, speed it up and keep your domain and Google listing intact.
