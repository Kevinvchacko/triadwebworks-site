Questions people ask

Something missing? Ask us on the free call.

Can you maintain a website someone else built?

Yes. We review the site first, tell you what needs fixing, and then take over updates and upkeep.

What does website maintenance include?

Small content changes, software and dependency updates, security checks, speed and SEO check-ups, and bug fixes on request. Larger new features are quoted separately.

How do I request a change?

Send it on WhatsApp or by email. We confirm what's needed and let you know when it's live.

Can you improve my website's SEO?

Yes. We fix the on-page basics, such as titles, descriptions, structured data, headings, speed and sitemaps, and help you set up Google Search Console and a Google Business Profile.

Is my website secure?

We check every site against a clear list: HTTPS and secure headers, protected forms, scanned dependencies and locked-down admin access. If anything fails, we tell you and fix it.
